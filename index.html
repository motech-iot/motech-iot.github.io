01 / IoT · EMBEDDED SYSTEMS

MoTech Aqua

An aquarium monitoring and management system combining embedded sensing, Wi-Fi connectivity and a mobile dashboard. The prototype is being tested in a real-world aquarium setting.

System overview

Sensor readings from an ESP32 are used by a connected monitoring setup, with MQTT, Firebase and a Flutter/Dart dashboard in the software stack.

Hardware & software

  • ESP32 with DS18B20 temperature sensing
  • HC-SR04 ultrasonic level sensing
  • Wi-Fi connectivity and MQTT messaging
  • Firebase and Flutter/Dart dashboard
  • Embedded C in the Arduino environment
Working prototype / real-world testing

02 / AUTOMATION · HARDWARE

EggLumina

An automated egg candling prototype exploring how controlled illumination and repeatable positioning could support inspection of multiple eggs.

Concept & mechanical design

An acrylic assembly carries the lighting and egg positions while a stepper-driven mechanism explores movement through the inspection sequence.

Hardware & control

  • Arduino Nano controller and LDR sensing
  • NEMA 17 stepper motor with A4988 driver
  • Acrylic mechanical assembly
  • Separate 5V and 12V power rails
Prototype / active development

03 / AUDIO · HARDWARE · PRODUCT DESIGN

MoTech Pure

An industrial portable speaker prototype combining a custom enclosure, multiple audio drivers and amplification paths with hands-on fabrication.

Enclosure & fabrication

The design explores a geometric steel enclosure with aluminum elements and wood accents. Mechanical development uses FreeCAD; this remains an ongoing physical prototype.

Audio hardware

  • JBL drivers and multiple amplifier paths
  • 4Ω / 10W speaker paths and PAM8403
  • Passive crossover
  • Steel enclosure, aluminum elements and wood accents
  • FreeCAD mechanical design
Physical prototype / ongoing development

BACKGROUND

Building across boundaries.

I’m Mohamed, an embedded systems engineer based in Zanzibar, Tanzania, and the founder-builder behind MoTech. MoTech is my independent engineering venture for exploring and building with embedded systems, IoT, electronics and physical products. I’m interested in the point where hardware and software meet: making a device sense, respond, connect and work as a complete system.

My projects are practical prototypes. I document what is being explored and where each build stands, without presenting work in progress as a finished product.

ENGINEERING FOCUS

Systems that touch the real world.

My work spans microcontrollers and sensors, firmware, wireless communication, dashboard interfaces, electronics integration, mechanical design and physical prototyping.

CURRENT LEARNING

I continue to develop through hands-on experiments in connected embedded systems, reliable hardware/software integration and the design and fabrication of physical products.
